## Description:

Helps authors improve Chinese and English academic prose through local style checks, text cleanup, guided rewriting, and checks that preserve numbers, citations, and terminology.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[zhaoxinghua09-cell](https://clawhub.ai/user/zhaoxinghua09-cell)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT

## Use Case:

Authors, researchers, and editors use this skill to review and improve academic drafts in Chinese or English while checking that essential facts and citations remain intact. It supports writing-quality review, not concealment of AI assistance or authorship misrepresentation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: A rewrite could change factual details or protected terms.

Mitigation: Review changes and run the included integrity checks against the original draft before use.

Risk: Style changes could be misused to conceal AI assistance or misrepresent authorship.

Mitigation: Use only on drafts you are authorized to edit and follow applicable disclosure and academic-integrity rules.
